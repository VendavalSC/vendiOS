vendiOS public beta

Minimal. Modern. Yours.

A Linux desktop where windows find their own place, everything moves like it’s alive, and it’s all ready the moment you log in.

Download the beta See what’s inside ›

Windows tile themselves. Every motion runs on springs. Every setting is one key away. And you never have to touch a config file.

Scratchpad

A shelf for everything else.

Send a window to the side of the screen and it keeps running there as a live card. The visualizer keeps dancing, the build keeps compiling. Click it, and it’s back.

super shift − send to the shelfsuper − show or hide it

Dashboard

One key. Everything else.

super D brings down your calendar, the weather, what’s playing, and the switches you reach for every day: Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, do not disturb, recording.

Launcher

Type. Enter. Done.

super space finds apps as you type, and searches the web when nothing matches. super K shows every shortcut on screen, so there’s nothing to memorise.

And so much more.

A snapshot before every update.

If an update ever breaks something, vendi rollback and a reboot put you right back where you were. Your files stay untouched.

Installs offline.

Every package is on the 1.9 GB image, AMD, Intel and NVIDIA drivers included.

Encrypted by default.

LUKS2 with argon2id. One passphrase at boot.

One command for it all.

vendi wifi, bt, display, update and about twenty more.

Themes that change everything.

Bar, terminal and apps recolour together, live. Or take the colours from your wallpaper.

Capture, record, copy text.

Screenshot an area, record the screen, or grab the text out of anything you can see.

Games in one command.

vendi game sets up Steam, Lutris, gamemode, gamescope and MangoHud.

Your phone, on your desktop.

Mirror an iPhone over AirPlay, or an Android phone over USB.

Still Arch, underneath.

pacman, the AUR and Arch’s own repositories work exactly the way you’d expect. vendiOS just makes the part you see feel finished.

A tour that makes you do it.

On first login, a short walkthrough waits for you to actually try each move.

Get vendiOS.

Free and open source. The first public beta is ready to try.
