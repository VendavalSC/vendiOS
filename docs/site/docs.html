Documentation

Everything on this page works on a fresh install. None of it needs a config file unless you want to change something.

Installing

  1. Download the ISO from the release page, along with the .sha256 file. To check it: sha256sum -c vendios-2026.09.24-x86_64.iso.sha256.
  2. Write it to a USB stick. On Linux: sudo dd if=vendios-2026.09.24-x86_64.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress oflag=sync. On Windows or macOS, Fedora Media Writer or balenaEtcher will do it.
  3. Turn off Secure Boot in your firmware settings and boot from the stick.
  4. The installer starts on its own. It asks for language, timezone and keyboard; which disk to use (the whole disk, free space, or your own partitions); btrfs or ext4; whether to encrypt; your user and hostname; which bar; and optional extras like swap and the yay AUR helper.
  5. Nothing is written to the disk until the last screen, where you confirm. Then reboot, log in, and you’re at the desktop.

Encryption is on by default: LUKS2 with argon2id. You type the passphrase once at boot. Pick btrfs if you want the automatic snapshots; on ext4 you don’t get them.

Building the ISO yourself

sudo pacman -S archiso limine xorriso
git clone https://github.com/VendavalSC/vendiOS
cd vendiOS
sudo ./build.sh --clean --offline   # ends up in out/

Leave out --offline for a much smaller ISO that downloads packages while it installs.

First login

A short tour opens the first time you log in. It doesn’t just show slides: it asks you to open a window, switch workspace and open the overview, and waits until you’ve actually done it. Skip it if you like; vendi welcome brings it back.

Three keys get you most of the way:

super spaceLaunch an app by typing its name
super returnOpen a terminal
super KShow every shortcut on screen

Then connect to Wi‑Fi with vendi wifi (or from the dashboard) and run sudo vendi update once.

Windows and tiling

New windows split the space with the ones already open, so nothing overlaps and nothing hides behind anything else. super H and super ctrl V decide whether the next one opens beside or below, and super T cycles through the other layouts.

The mouse works too. Hold super and drag a window with the left button to pick it up; the other tiles show where it’ll land and move out of the way when you let go. Drop it on the middle of another window and they become tabs. super and the right button resizes. super shift space lets a window float on top like on a normal desktop, and the same keys put it back.

  • Tabs. super G groups windows into one tile with tabs across the top, like a browser. Press it again to move to the next tab; super shift G splits them up.
  • Pin. super P keeps a window on every workspace.
  • Blur and opacity. super shift B toggles blur behind a window, super shift O steps through opacity levels.

Touchscreens work as a mouse: tap to click, drag to move, long-press for right-click.

The scratchpad shelf

The scratchpad isn’t a hidden drawer. super shift − sends the window you’re in flying into a card on a shelf at the left of the screen, and it keeps running there as a live miniature.

  • Click a card to swap that window back onto the screen in place of the one you’re using.
  • Shift-click a card to bring it back next to what you’re doing instead.
  • Drag a card out of the shelf and drop it where you want it.
  • super = swaps in the top card; super − hides or shows the shelf.

Workspaces and monitors

Workspaces are made when you go to them, so there’s no fixed number to set up. super O zooms out to show all of them at once; click one to go there.

Plug in a second monitor and it turns on next to the first. To change which side it’s on, its scale or its resolution, run vendi display, pick the monitor, and pick what to change.

The bar and dashboard

You pick a bar in the installer and can swap at any time with vendi bar pro or vendi bar classic. Nothing else changes when you switch.

Pro sits at the top centre like a notch and grows when something happens: you change the volume or brightness, or the battery runs low. Click it while music is playing for the controls.

Classic is a thin line of text and icons along the top, for when you want the screen to stay out of your way.

super D opens the dashboard: a calendar, the weather, what’s playing, and quick switches for do not disturb, recording, Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, audio, screenshots, a colour picker and the lock screen. The tabs along the top hold system stats, tools, wallpapers, config, monitors and the screensaver.

Screenshots and recording

print takes the whole screen. super shift S lets you drag a box and puts the picture on the clipboard, ready to paste. Screenshots are saved to ~/Pictures/Screenshots.

super shift R starts recording the screen and the same keys stop it. Use vendi record area for part of the screen, or add --audio to record sound. Recordings go to ~/Videos/Recordings.

super shift T reads the text inside a box you drag, which is handy for error messages in screenshots or videos. super V opens everything you’ve copied recently.

Themes and wallpapers

vendi theme recolours the bar, the terminal and GTK apps together, with no restart. It ships with mocha, latte, gruvbox, nord, tokyonight, everforest, mono and think. There’s also dynamic, which picks the colours from your wallpaper.

Wallpapers go in ~/Pictures/Wallpapers, and the dashboard has a picker for them. Give vendi wallpaper dynamic set a day picture and a night picture (and a dusk one if you want), and it swaps between them over the day. With vendi appearance auto, apps go light or dark to match whichever wallpaper is showing.

Lock screen and idle

super esc locks. The screen also locks itself after 10 minutes of not being touched and turns off a minute later; any key or mouse movement wakes it. Closing the lid locks it before the laptop sleeps.

If your laptop has a fingerprint reader, run vendi fingerprint once and you can unlock with a finger instead of typing.

For something to look at while idle, put videos in ~/Videos/Screensavers and choose one with vendi screensaver.

Updates and snapshots

sudo vendi update finds the fastest mirrors and upgrades everything. It’s pacman -Syu with the mirror step done for you. If the update included a new version of vendiWM, log out and back in to use it.

On btrfs, every pacman run takes a snapshot before and after. If an update leaves you with something broken:

sudo vendi rollback       # lists snapshots, asks for a number
sudo vendi rollback 42    # or give it straight away

Reboot afterwards and the system is back to how it was at that snapshot. Your home folder isn’t rolled back, so your files stay as they are.

vendi snapshot takes one by hand, before you try something risky. sudo vendi clean clears old packages out of the cache when the disk gets full.

The config file

Everything works without one. When you want to change something, create ~/.config/vendi/vendiwm.kdl and put in only what you want to change: anything you leave out keeps its default. super ctrl R reloads it.

binds {
    bind "super+b" "spawn chromium"      // replaces the default super+b
    bind "super+m" "spawn spotify"       // adds a new one
}

input {
    keyboard-layout "us,es"              // super+i switches
    repeat-delay 200
    repeat-rate 25
    natural-scroll true
    tap-to-click true
    focus-follows-mouse false
    mod-key "super"                      // or "alt", "ctrl", "caps"
}

theme {
    gap 10
    margin 14
    radius 12
    blur true
    smart-gaps false                     // true: no gaps with one window
}

idle {
    lock-after 600                       // seconds, 0 turns it off
    screen-off-after 660
}

output "DP-1" {
    mode "2560x1440@165"
    vrr "fullscreen"
}

mod-key "caps" turns Caps Lock into a second Super key, which helps on small keyboards where the real one is awkward to reach. Monitor names like DP-1 are shown in vendi display.

Window rules

Rules decide where a window opens and how it looks. Match by app id (exact), title (any part of it) or the program’s name, then say what to do with it:

rules {
    rule app-id="firefox" workspace=2
    rule app-id="pavucontrol" float=true
    rule title="Picture-in-Picture" float=true opacity=0.95
    rule process="mpv" fullscreen=true
}

The actions are workspace, float, opacity (0 to 1) and fullscreen. Rules go in the same vendiwm.kdl.

Web apps

Some things only exist as websites. vendi webapp add gives one its own window, its own icon in the launcher and no browser toolbar:

vendi webapp add whatsapp
vendi webapp add "Linear" https://linear.app
vendi webapp list

WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Notion, Figma and Gmail are built in by name. For anything else, give it a name and a URL.

Phone mirroring

vendi phone shows your phone’s screen in a window on the desktop. Run vendi phone setup once first; the iPhone part comes from the AUR, so it needs yay.

  • iPhone: run vendi phone, then on the phone open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and pick your computer. Both need to be on the same Wi‑Fi.
  • Android: turn on USB debugging, plug in the cable, run vendi phone.

Gaming

vendi game installs Steam, Lutris, gamemode, gamescope and MangoHud, and turns on the 32‑bit repository they need. With yay installed it also adds Heroic, ProtonUp-Qt and Moonlight.

Start a game with vendi game run <command> to get gamemode and the performance overlay. For Windows games in Steam, turn on Steam Play for all titles, and install Proton-GE from ProtonUp-Qt if a game needs it.

Dev setup

vendi dev installs Neovim with a LazyVim setup that follows your theme, plus lazygit, mise (for Node, Python, Go and Rust versions), starship, zellij, zoxide, eza and bat. Run nvim once afterwards with internet on; it downloads its plugins the first time.

When something breaks

  • After an update: sudo vendi rollback, pick the snapshot from before it, reboot.
  • The desktop won’t start: press ctrl alt F2 for a text login, and look at ~/logs/vendi-session.log. The log from the previous login is next to it as .log.1.
  • The bar vanished: it restarts itself; if it doesn’t, log out and back in.
  • A key doesn’t work: super K shows what’s actually bound. If you’ve got a vendiwm.kdl, it might be overriding it.

To report it, run vendi report. It packs the logs and a hardware summary into one file. Attach that to an issue on GitHub and say what you were doing when it happened.

What isn’t done

It’s a beta, and mostly one person’s work. What I know about:

  • Secure Boot isn’t supported yet. It’s on the list.
  • Fractional scaling (125%, 150%) on high-DPI screens hasn’t had much testing.
  • The local AI assistant you’ll see in the code isn’t on this ISO yet.

So far it’s been tested on AMD and Intel CPUs, AMD and NVIDIA graphics, two monitors, and an Intel Chromebook. Keep a backup of anything you care about.

Questions

Can I install it next to Windows?
Yes. Pick “free space” or your own partitions in the installer instead of the whole disk.
Is it still Arch underneath?
Yes. pacman and the AUR work as normal, and updates come straight from Arch’s own repositories.
Can I use my own apps instead of Firefox and Files?
Install what you like with pacman or yay, then point the key at it in the config file.
Does it work with a normal mouse, not just the keyboard?
Yes. Hold super and drag to move or resize windows, and click anything in the bar. The keys are there for speed, not because you need them.
